Who we are

Food stories, useful ideas and local perspective.

Dhanbad Food Guide explores food, dining, culinary traditions and local food culture in and around Dhanbad, Jharkhand.

Our editorial approach

We aim to publish useful, original and informative content. Our guides focus on practical considerations—such as menu variety, planning, comfort, hygiene questions and food traditions—so readers can make choices that suit their own needs.

Ownership disclosure

This publication is operated by Vishhal Family Restaurant, Dhanbad, Jharkhand. Our editorial content explores food, dining, culinary traditions and local food culture. We aim to provide useful, original and informative content to readers. Vishhal Family Restaurant may be featured in relevant articles. Such features should be understood in the context of our ownership and commercial interests. We do not claim to be an independent third-party review platform.

About Vishhal Family Restaurant

Vishhal Family Restaurant is a family dining destination in Dhanbad serving vegetarian and non-vegetarian food, including Indian favourites, tandoor, Chinese dishes, biryani and snacks.
